Student feedback

What students told us.

We showed the Smart Glove to students at NISH in Thiruvananthapuram and St. Louis College for the Deaf in Chennai, then asked what they thought. This page sets out what we heard and what we plan to do about it.

NISH, ThiruvananthapuramSt. Louis College for the Deaf, Chennai

What we heard

Some were happy. Some had ideas. Some wanted in.

Happy with the product

A tool built for them

Some students were happy with the glove. It gives a signer a way to be understood by people who do not know sign language, which is the problem we set out to solve.

Suggestions

Different sign languages, and a more comfortable fit

Some students pointed out that sign languages differ by language and region, and the glove must account for that. Others said it should be easier and more comfortable to wear.

Wanting to take part

Students asked to join us

Some students asked to be part of the project and its development, not only to use the finished glove.

Our plan

How we will respond.

Every suggestion goes into the next version of the glove.

Support different sign languages

Students told us sign languages are tied to different languages and regions.

  • Let the user choose their sign language before they start signing.
  • Record and train gestures with signers from each community, so the vocabulary comes from the people who use it.
  • Keep the language choice that already exists in the web app, so the text appears in the language the user wants.

Make it more wearable and comfortable

Students said the glove could be more comfortable to wear.

  • Keep reducing weight, one of our original goals.
  • Tidy the wiring and make the electronics smaller.
  • Test the fit with different hand sizes and with longer wearing time, and change the design based on what students tell us.

Bring students into the project

Students asked to join the project and its development.

  • Give students a real role in testing the glove and shaping its design.
  • Invite signers to contribute gestures and review the results.
  • Keep in touch with each institute as the glove improves.

These are planned next steps. They are not features of the current prototype.
